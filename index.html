Authentic Nepali Heritage

Hospitality Rooted in the Mountains

Managed under the care of proprietor Ex. Army Yubaraj KC, Lucky Twins Annapurna View Point Hotel & Restaurant was founded on a simple Nepali belief: Atithi Devo Bhava (The Guest is God).

Perched high on the Kaski ridge overlooking the majestic snowline of the Annapurna Massif, we provide weary road travelers, trekking parties, and visiting families with a peaceful home away from home. Here, nothing is rushed: curries are slow-simmered over local firewood, spices are stone-ground, and the mountain breeze refreshes every spirit.

"Whether you come for a quick cup of cardamom milk tea, a plate of steamed chicken momo, or stay overnight under starry mountain skies, our doors are warmly open."

— Ex. Army Yubaraj KC, Proprietor
